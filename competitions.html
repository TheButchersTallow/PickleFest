Pickle Fest Competitions — Join the Fun!

Pickle Fest brings the heat with fan-favorite competitions that everyone can enjoy! From pickle eating and juice holding to canning, pet costumes, juice drinking, and the Mascot Mayhem Dance Off, there's a challenge for every competitor. All competition registration happens on the day of the festival — just show up, sign up, and compete for bragging rights and prizes. Whether you're a serious contender or just here for the laughs, our competitions are a highlight of Pickle Fest Texarkana.

Pickle Canning Competition & 2025 Champs

Got a secret family recipe that deserves a blue ribbon? Bring your best canned creation starring pickles — sweet, spicy, classic, or creative — and compete for top dill honors. Congrats to B S Farms, our Official 2025 Pickle Fest Champs! Think you can take the crown in 2026? Sign up the day of the festival and find out. 🥒🏆

Canning Sponsor & 2025 Pickle Fest Champs

Pickle Juice Holding
Competition

Think you've got the grit to hold the brine? Step up and grab a massive stein filled with pickle juice and see how long your arm can hang in there. It sounds easy… until the brine starts burning and the crowd starts cheering. Last person still holding their stein wins the ultimate bragging rights!

Pickle Juice Drinking Competition

Get your belly ready and your taste buds brined up! Step up and see who has what it takes to drink down pickle juice the fastest. It's salty, it's tangy, and it's guaranteed to get the crowd cheering. Think you've got the stomach for it? Come give it a shot—more details coming soon! 🥒🥤

Official Sponsor for Both Pickle Juice Competitions

Dress Your Pet Like a Pickle Competition

Bring your furry, feathered, or even scaly friends out to Pickle Fest and show off their best pickle-inspired look! Dress your pet up like a pickle (or anything delightfully briny) and parade them in front of the crowd for a chance to win bragging rights for the most creative, cutest, or most dill-lightful costume. If your pet has personality and a pickle outfit, this is their moment to shine! 🥒🐾

Official Competition Sponsor

Mascot Mayhem Dance Off

Think you've got the moves to match the brine? Hit the floor for the Mascot Mayhem Dance Off — a high-energy dance competition where pickle spirit meets showstopping style! Bring your best routine, your wildest pickle energy, and get ready to compete in front of a cheering festival crowd. Solo dancers, duos, and groups are all welcome. Judges will be looking for creativity, crowd energy, and pure Pickle Fest fun. Sign up the day of the festival and show Texarkana what you've got! 🥒💃🕺
