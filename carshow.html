Gas & Gherkins Car Show

Polish up the chrome and roll on into Pickle Fest! Bring your ride out to be part of the Gas & Gherkins Car Show, where classic cars, hot rods, and cool cruisers will line the streets for a day of horsepower and hometown pride. Vehicles will be judged, and more details on categories and registration will be announced later this summer. Until then—start shining those wheels and get ready to show off your ride! 🚗🥒

Categories & Registration

Classic cars, hot rods, and cool cruisers will line the streets of Downtown Texarkana for a day of horsepower and hometown pride. More details on categories and registration will be announced later this summer. Follow us on Facebook and Instagram for updates. Until then—start shining those wheels!

Judging & Prizes

Vehicles will be judged, and top entries will receive recognition and prizes. Stay tuned to our social media for the full lineup of award categories. We can't wait to see what rolls in! 🚗🥒
