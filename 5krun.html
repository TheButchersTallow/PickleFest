Run For The Spear Of It 5K

Lace up and hit the streets of downtown Texarkana before Pickle Fest kicks off! The Run For The Spear Of It 5K is a 3.1-mile route through downtown for runners and walkers of all ages and paces. Get a little sweat in before the brine and fun begin, then cross the finish line and enjoy the rest of Pickle Fest with food, vendors, competitions, and live music.
